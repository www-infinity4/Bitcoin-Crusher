# Bitcoin Crusher — Infinity Product Engine

## Product position

Bitcoin Crusher is not only a slot or spinner game. Each spin begins a guided product-building session. The output is a usable website, world, research package, storefront, media experience, companion configuration, or other user-controlled digital product.

The spinner supplies creative constraints and combinations. The AI turns those combinations into a coherent build. The user reviews, edits, versions, publishes, and may connect the finished creation to the Infinity network.

## Core loop

1. User chooses a goal or starts with an open spin.
2. Bitcoin Crusher spins categories such as audience, style, subject, business model, media type, research direction, companion role, and world theme.
3. The system generates a build brief before creating files.
4. Research agents gather current public information from approved internet sources.
5. Builder agents produce a preview and editable project files.
6. The user changes any element through small Star edit portals.
7. Every save creates a reversible version.
8. The user exports, hosts, publishes, or connects the product to an Infinity world.
9. Real usage, remixes, and qualified adoption are recorded separately from game activity.

## Real internet research

Research must use server-side connectors or approved APIs. The browser must not contain private API keys.

Every research result should retain:

- source URL and publisher;
- retrieval time;
- short supporting excerpt or structured fact;
- confidence and freshness;
- copyright and reuse status;
- whether the result is fact, estimate, opinion, or generated suggestion.

The AI must not invent sources, prices, partnerships, balances, downloads, or product availability.

## Product outputs

A spin can create:

- a responsive website;
- an Infinity entertainment world;
- a storefront or auction presentation;
- a research report and evidence library;
- a radio or media app theme;
- a creator portfolio;
- an advertising campaign concept;
- a companion personality and knowledge pack;
- an educational demonstration;
- an editable prototype package.

Each output receives a permanent project ID and editable display name. Themes, fonts, labels, layouts, and companion identities remain user-controlled.

## Bitcoin and Infinity accounting

Bitcoin Crusher must not claim to mine, scrape, seize, crush, convert, or harvest real Bitcoin merely by running JavaScript or collecting Bitcoin information.

Real Bitcoin can enter the system only through a legitimate and verifiable source such as:

- a user-authorized Bitcoin payment;
- mining performed by hardware controlled by the account holder;
- an approved affiliate or advertising payment;
- a donation or sponsorship;
- a licensed exchange or payment processor;
- other documented revenue received by the platform.

The system may visualize Bitcoin research or use Bitcoin as a creative game symbol, but simulated winnings are not real Bitcoin.

Infinity issuance must remain separate from Bitcoin revenue. Infinity units may be issued only under a written policy with:

- an authorized issuance event;
- a reason and recipient;
- a maximum or budget;
- a signed ledger entry;
- independent reconciliation;
- reversal and dispute controls;
- no direct model access to minting keys.

AI may calculate, recommend, and prepare a proposed distribution. AI must not silently print balances or settle debt without policy authorization and an auditable transaction.

## Ledgers

Maintain separate ledgers for:

- spins and generated briefs;
- product files and versions;
- research sources;
- public creation usage;
- StarCoin events;
- Avatar Coin events;
- Infinity issuance and transfers;
- Bitcoin deposits or revenue;
- fiat obligations;
- partner and creator settlement;
- refunds, reversals, fraud reviews, and disputes.

A visual game result must never mutate a financial ledger directly.

## Trust and anti-corruption design

The trustworthy approach is not unlimited money creation by a trusted person or model. It is constrained issuance that everyone can inspect.

Required controls:

- append-only signed transaction history;
- role separation for proposing, approving, and executing issuance;
- per-partner and system-wide limits;
- duplicate-event and replay protection;
- identity-bound balances rather than copyable bearer files;
- automatic anomaly detection;
- human-visible receipts and explanations;
- emergency freeze and recovery procedures;
- external accounting review before studio settlement.

No system should be described as impossible to hack. The goal is to make attacks difficult, limit damage, detect unauthorized actions, invalidate stolen credentials, and restore the authoritative state.

## Connection to StarQuest

Bitcoin Crusher should reuse the Infinity foundation being developed through StarQuest:

- user-controlled worlds;
- renameable companions;
- small Star edit portals;
- reversible versions;
- public creation chain;
- verified usage and creator attribution;
- separate participation, creator, and settlement ledgers;
- studio and partner worlds;
- single-page world switching.

A completed Bitcoin Crusher product can become a new world or a component inside an existing world.

## Alien Radio handoff

Alien Radio is the next connected product after the Bitcoin Crusher foundation is stable. Its redesign should use an app-like close-up interface with:

- persistent player controls;
- stations and discoveries;
- Harvest Feed;
- research articles with citations and update times;
- user worlds and themes;
- renameable companion;
- Star edit portals and version history;
- legitimate revenue events separated from game or feed activity.

Harvest Feed may report verified Bitcoin-related revenue or research. It must not imply that listening, browsing, or scraping automatically creates Bitcoin. Any conversion into Infinity requires an authorized exchange or issuance policy and a signed settlement record.
